USE CASES

One crew,
every kind of bad day.

AidMesh is not tied to one disaster type. Anything that produces a flood of raw, multilingual, contradictory reports is exactly what the crew eats.

WHERE IT HELPSSix scenarios, one pipeline

🌊

Flash floods

Trapped families, blocked roads, shelters asking for supplies. This is our recorded demo: Alexandria, six reports, five real incidents, one rumour killed.

🏚️

Earthquakes

Collapse reports arrive by the hundred within minutes. Verify merges duplicates from the same building, Triage puts voids with survivors first.

🔥

Wildfires

Fast-moving fronts mean fast-moving rumours. The crew tracks which evacuation reports are corroborated and which are recycled old photos.

⚡

Blackouts and grid failure

Hospitals on generators, dialysis patients on the clock. Dispatch names fuel and biomedical crews with the safe first action, like in our demo's Sidi Gaber clinic incident.

🚗

Mass-casualty road incidents

Conflicting casualty counts from bystanders get a RUMOUR or UNVERIFIED label until a second source lands, so ambulances go where they are actually needed.

📢

Rumour control

Alert issues bilingual public corrections. In the demo run it publicly defused a fake "dam collapse, thousands dead" post in Arabic and English.

WALKTHROUGHA flood night in Alexandria, minute by minute

This is the actual recorded run that ships with the repo. Follow one night through the pipeline.

1

Reports pour in

A call in Egyptian Arabic about a family trapped in Manshiya. A tweet in English describing the same family. An SMS from a shelter in Agami. A post about a live wire near Stanley Bridge. A viral dam-collapse rumour. A clinic losing power with 6 dialysis patients.

2

LangChain structures the chaos

Every report becomes a clean incident object: need, location, people affected, life-threatening flag, language. Arabic and English treated as first-class citizens.

3

The crew takes over

Verify merges the two Manshiya reports into one VERIFIED incident and marks the dam post RUMOUR, backed by a 0-100 credibility score. Triage geocodes every incident, checks the flood-risk forecast and ranks: trapped family P1, clinic P2, shelter P3. Dispatch names the nearest real units with distances: Montaza Civil Defence 4.5 km, Smouha electricity unit 1.2 km, Moharam Bek water depot 12.9 km.

4

The public hears the truth

Alert sends bilingual guidance to responders and a public correction: the dam-collapse story is false, follow official sources only.

WHO IT IS FORBuilt for real coordination rooms

🏛️ Civil defence and municipalities

A first-pass triage layer on top of hotlines and social media during the first chaotic hours.

🤝 NGOs and volunteer networks

Turn scattered WhatsApp and Facebook posts into a ranked, deduplicated needs list for shelter supply runs.

📰 Newsrooms

A verification assistant that labels what is corroborated, unverified or rumour before anything is published.

Honest scope: AidMesh assists human coordinators. Verification labels, not blind trust, are the safety feature, and a human keeps the final veto on every dispatch.
