AGENT 03 · CREWAI

🚁 The Resource
Dispatcher

Priority without a plan is just anxiety. The Dispatcher turns every ranked incident into a resource, a first safe action, and the hazard to check before anyone moves.

Role: Resource DispatcherGoal: a safe plan per incidentTools: geocode_place · nearest_resources · weather_flood_risk

WHAT IT DOESPlans a responder can actually use

🚤

Named resources, real distances

nearest_resources + geocode_place turn "send help" into "Montaza Civil Defence Station, 4.5 km" for the trapped family and "Electricity Emergency Unit - Smouha, 1.2 km" for the dialysis clinic.

🦺

First safe action, always

Never "go". Always "isolate the live wire before any personnel enter the water". The first instruction is the one that keeps the rescuer alive.

⚠️

Hazard callouts

Current strength, conducting water, fuel flammability, unstable embankments. Every plan names what could kill the rescue.

🗂️

Registry, not imagination

Distances come from a resource registry (a sample of 8 Alexandria entries in this demo), never from the model making up a station. Swap in a real civil-defence registry and the same crew works anywhere.

With Dispatch
