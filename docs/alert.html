📣 The Community
Communicator

The last agent in the crew speaks to the public. Bilingual alerts for responders and residents, and corrections that defuse rumours before they become stampedes.

Role: Community CommunicatorGoal: the public hears the truthArabic + English

WHAT IT DOESWords calibrated for a crisis

🌍

Truly bilingual

Not translation as an afterthought. Arabic and English alerts written for their audiences, matching the languages the reports arrived in.

📱

Channel-ready

Short, SMS-length messages. No jargon, no paragraphs. What is happening, what to do, what not to do.

🧯

Rumour correction

When Verify kills a rumour, Alert publicly defuses it: "No confirmed dam collapse. Stay calm, await official updates." In both languages.

🎯

Right message, right people

Responder instructions carry operational detail. Public alerts carry calm and direction. They are never the same text.
