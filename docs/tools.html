THE FIVE TOOLSWhat the crew can actually do

🧪 credibility_score

A rule-based 0 to 100 rumour score with its reasons in plain language: extreme casualty claims, vague locations, missing corroboration all cost points. Called by the Verifier.

rule-basedexplainable

🌐 detect_language

Tells Arabic from English from mixed, so a bilingual report stream is routed and counted correctly. Called by the Verifier.

ar / en / mixed

📍 geocode_place

Turns "Sidi Gaber clinic" into real coordinates through OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Called by Triage and Dispatch.

OpenStreetMap

🌧️ weather_flood_risk

Next-24h rain and river-discharge trend from Open-Meteo, so triage knows whether the water is still rising. Called by Triage and Dispatch.

Open-Meteo

🚑 nearest_resources

Nearest hospital, dialysis, rescue, shelter, electric or water unit by distance. Called by Dispatch.

sample registry: 8 Alexandria entries

Honesty label: the resource registry is sample data (8 entries for Alexandria) standing in for a real civil-defence registry. Geocoding and weather are live public APIs.
