The Triage
Lead

Everything is urgent to someone. The Triage Lead decides what is urgent to everyone: a hard P1, P2, P3 ranking by threat to life, with the reasoning attached.

Role: Triage LeadGoal: the right thing firstTools: geocode_place · weather_flood_risk

AGENT CONSOLEStatus, tools and output

WHAT IT DOESRuthless, explainable ordering

Life threat first

A trapped family with an elder struggling to breathe lands at P1. Nothing outranks breathing.

⏳

Time-window reasoning

Six dialysis patients with three hours of generator fuel left are P2: not dying this minute, dead if ignored. The clock is part of the ranking.

Weather-aware ranking

geocode_place pins every incident on the map and weather_flood_risk reads the next 24 hours of rain and river discharge, so a shelter request during rising water is ranked with the forecast in mind.

Every rank has a reason

No black box. Each line of output says why an incident sits where it sits, so a human can override it intelligently.

WHY IT MATTERSOrder is a decision someone must own




Before AidMesh
Whoever shouts loudest in the coordination chat gets the boat. Quiet, time-critical cases like the clinic wait until it is too late.


With Triage
A defensible order, in writing, in seconds. Commanders can disagree with it, and that argument is exactly the one worth having.
